What's inside

Everything your money touches, in one place.

Start with a single account. Grow into a full shop. Ghollak keeps up.

01Accounts & transactions

See all your money at a glance.

Accounts in any currency, a daily digest of what's coming up, savings goals that fill as you go, and recurring transactions that record themselves.

  • Multiple accounts and currencies
  • A savings goal for every account
  • Recurring transactions and transfers

02People

Know exactly who owes whom.

A ledger for every person: what you gave, what came back and what's still open. Settle up in a tap and share a clean statement.

  • Debts and receivables per person
  • Settlements recorded in your accounts
  • Shareable statements

03Store

Run your shop from your pocket.

Products, stock, purchases, sales and returns, with a barcode scanner in the camera. Ghollak works out average cost, profit and what your inventory is worth.

  • Barcode scanning
  • Sales, purchase and return invoices
  • Stock value and gross profit

04Checks & reminders

Never miss a due date again.

Track checks you've received and issued, get a heads-up before they're due, and archive them once they clear. Set reminders for anything else, too.

  • Received and issued checks
  • Due-date notifications your way
  • Reminders for anything

05Reports & export

Reports you'd proudly send.

Charts that show where the money goes, and PDF or Excel exports with your logo, title, colors and the columns you choose.

  • Pie, bar, line and trend charts
  • PDF and Excel with your branding
  • Every past export, one tap away
